Introduction¶

You will now work in the same groups of 3–4 students you used to create your mind maps.

Each group will receive a specific scenario and budget:

  • Laptop for school use – up to €700

  • Laptop for gaming – up to €3000

  • Laptop for basic office work – up to €1000

  • Laptop for video editing – up to €2500

  • Laptop for programming – up to €2000

Roles in the Team

  • Researcher: finds necessary information using the group mind map and relevant online sources.

  • Recorder: writes down the group’s answers and checks they meet the required specifications.

  • Technician: finds the chosen laptop in an online store and checks whether it matches the criteria.

  • Presenter: prepares a short presentation with the specifications and explains the group’s choice.

If the group has three members, combine the roles of Recorder and Presenter.

Napomena

While each member of the group has an individual role, collaboration is key — everyone supports each other in all stages of the task. You are accountable for completing your role effectively and on time, but at the same time, you contribute to the group’s overall outcome.
