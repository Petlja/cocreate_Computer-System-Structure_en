Assessment Overview¶

Your work will be evaluated according to three components: • Mind map creation – structure, content, clarity, and collaboration (10 points) • Independent task – accuracy and understanding in the quiz (10 points) • Group work – contribution to teamwork, reasoning, and participation in discussion (10 points)
