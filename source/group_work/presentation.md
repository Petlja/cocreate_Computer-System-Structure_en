# Presentation

## Task
### (30 minutes)

Now that you are in your groups, start working on finding the laptop that best fits your assigned scenario. You have a total of **30 minutes** for the group work. Of that time, **20 minutes** are allocated for research and team discussion, and the remaining **10 minutes** for entering data into Google Slides and preparing the presentation.

**Group Task**

Design a laptop configuration for your assigned scenario and answer the following questions:

-	What type of processor do you need – how many cores and what approximate clock speed?
-	How much RAM is sufficient for your purpose?
-	What type and capacity of storage do you need?
-	What additional features must the laptop have for your purpose (e.g. graphics card, ports, operating system, screen size and resolution...)?
-	Based on the agreed specifications, find a laptop model in an online store that best meets your requirements. Try to get the best value for the available budget.
-	Based on the agreed specifications, prepare one slide in the shared Google Slides document. The slide should include:
    -	Group name, laptop purpose, and budget
    -	Technical specifications (processor, RAM, storage, OS, and other details)
    -	A photo of the chosen laptop and a link to the store
-	Prepare a short presentation (3–4 minutes) in which you will explain:
    -	Why did you choose these specifications?
    -	Why this specific laptop?
    -	How does it meet the needs of the user in the given scenario?
