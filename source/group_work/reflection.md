# Reflection


Now that you have finished everything, take a few minutes to think about your own work and your team's work. There is nothing to fill in — read the following statements and for each one, honestly consider how well it applies to your team. Where were you strong? Where is there room for improvement?

1.	During team work, we were all equal and respected each other.
2.	We maintained a good atmosphere even when we thought differently.
3.	We were all responsible for the group's work.
4.	We helped each other so that everyone could contribute to the group's work.
5.	We tried to include every team member in decision-making.
6.	We considered different ideas in order to choose the best solution.
7.	We developed ideas, discussed them, and designed solutions together.
8.	We used our time wisely to successfully complete the task.
9.	We monitored our progress and thought about next steps.

If you feel that some of these principles were not sufficiently present in your team, think about what you personally could do differently in your next team project.

4. Fully applies to my team

3. We were all responsible for the group's work.
4. We helped each other so that everyone could contribute to the group's work.

7. We developed ideas, discussed them, and designed solutions together.

#### Suggested discussion after the questionnaire

Review the results with students. If the average score for a particular principle is below 3, it is a sign that the team should work on improving that aspect of teamwork in the future. Encourage students to be specific: what exactly happened, why, and what each team member could do differently next time.
