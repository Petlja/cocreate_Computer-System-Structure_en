As part of reviewing knowledge about computer components, you have created mind maps in teams. Today you continue with tasks through which you will apply your knowledge of computer components, and your team's mind map will help you.

In the next activity, you will work independently on tasks on the computer.

Your task is to analyse four different computer configurations and, using your group's mind map, answer the questions. Be careful – there may be one or more correct answers.

**Time limit: 15 minutes**    

After submitting your answers, you will receive automatic feedback about your results, which we will then discuss together.
