# Mind map

**Instructions for creating the mind map**

1.	Form groups of 3–4 students, according to your preference.

2.	Open [Miro](https://www.miro.com) and start creating a collaborative mind map:

    a. All group members register on the Miro platform using their school email address.

    b. One member of the group creates a mind map, copies it to a new map linked to their account, and invites the other group members to collaborate.
3.	Review the sample mind map published on Petlja, and based on the knowledge from previous lessons, create your own group mind map. The map should show all discussed devices and their classifications, applying what you have already learned.

4.	Optionally, add short notes (e.g. what each component does and its main features) and images or icons.

5.	If you need additional learning support, you can adjust the appearance of your map: use larger fonts, clear colours, icons, or visual markers.

6.	When finished, export the map as an image and submit it via Petlja in the next activity.
