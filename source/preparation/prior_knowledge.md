# Required prior knowledge

**Before this lesson you have already learned to:** 

-   name and group the basic components of a computer according to their purpose,

-   identify different input and output devices and types of data storage media,

-   describe the functional units of a computer and explain the basic principle of how it works,

-   distinguish between hardware and software,

-   distinguish between system and application software and provide examples of programs.

These concepts were gradually explored in previous lessons. For homework, you will now organise these ideas into a mind map. 
Your mind map will serve as support for individual and group tasks in the next lesson.

Before starting group work, review the rules of successful collaboration:
-   We are all equal and treat each other with respect.
-   We maintain a positive atmosphere, even when we think differently.
-   Each of us is responsible for the group’s work.
-   We help everyone contribute to the group task.
-   We try to include all members in decision-making.
-    We consider different ideas to choose the best solution.
-   We develop ideas, discuss them, and design solutions together.
-   We use time wisely to complete the task.
-   We monitor our progress and think about next steps.

In the next activity, you will find instructions for creating a mind map.
